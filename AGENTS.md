## Notifications and alerts are toasts

Every notification or alert is a toast at the top right that closes itself after 8 seconds. That covers anything reporting an outcome or event: "You signed out.", "Changes saved.", "Request sent.", "Could not save: …".

- Call `toast(message, tone)` from `src/components/ui/toast.tsx`. Tone is `"ok"`, `"error"` or `"info"`. For a server result, use `toast(r.message, r.ok ? "ok" : "error")`. In the Administration console, `notify` and `useAct` call the same toast.
- `<Toaster />` is mounted once in `src/app/layout.tsx`. Don't mount another. For an outcome, don't build a local toast, a banner, a `Notice`, or a status line inside the page.
- Don't override the timing or position. `TOAST_MS` is 8000 ms for every toast. The timer pauses while the pointer or keyboard focus is on a toast, and each toast has a close button. Toasts stack newest first, up to four. A repeated message restarts its timer instead of adding a copy. They sit 16px from the top right (full width minus 16px gutters on phones) and show above modal dialogs.
- When a message arrives with a redirect (for example `/signin?ended=signed-out`), show it as a toast on arrival and remove the parameter from the URL.
- These stay inline and are not toasts: validation for the field someone is filling in (shown next to that field), standing page context (read-only, locked, scope or data warnings), the admin-set site notice banner, the bell's notification inbox, and a form's own confirmation screen that replaces the form after it is sent.

## Form pickers are the Hub's own, never the browser's

Dropdowns, date pickers and number steppers use the branded components in `src/components/ui/pickers.tsx`, so they look the same in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, on desktop and phone. Don't add a native `<select>`, `<input type="date">` or `<input type="number">`, and don't hand-build another popup list or calendar.

- `Select` replaces `<select>`. Keep the `<option>` and `<optgroup>` children and the `select` / `select--sm` class. `onChange` receives the value, not an event: `<Select className="select" value={v} onChange={setV}>`. Give an `<option>` `data-depth={n}` to indent it in a tree. `defaultValue`, `name`, `required`, `disabled`, `placeholder` and `aria-label` work as they do on the native control.
- `DateField` replaces `<input type="date">`. Its value is an ISO date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) or `""`. It takes `min` and `max`, and `onChange` receives the date. The calendar has month and year views, so a birthday stays a few clicks away.
- `NumberField` replaces `<input type="number">`, with the Hub's steppers in place of the browser's spin buttons. `onChange` receives a number.
- Lists and calendars open as manual popovers. They show above modal drawers, are never clipped by a scrolling panel, and take their colours from the surface they open on through `--pick-*` variables (`src/styles/pickers.css`). A new surface with its own palette sets those variables; it doesn't restyle the popover.
- Keep dropdown and calendar scrolling inside `.pick__scroll`, inset within the padded `.pick` shell. The shell clips to its rounded corners; the inner region owns overflow and the scrollbar. Never put the scrollbar on the outer rounded shell, where its thumb or track can overshoot the corners at either end.
- Keyboard and screen reader support is built in: arrow keys, Home/End, Page Up/Down, type-to-jump and Esc (which closes the picker, not the drawer around it). Keep the label around the picker or pass `aria-label`.
- A `required` picker reports a missing value through the Hub's field messages (below), pointing at its trigger. `name` still sends the value with a native form.
- Checkboxes and radios stay native, tinted with `accent-color` or drawn as the `.choice` cards and `Switch`. File inputs stay `sr-only` behind a branded button or drop zone.

## Field validation messages are the Hub's own

This rule applies to every form field throughout the app, in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, on desktop and mobile. The browser's validation bubble and native validation hover tooltip ("Please fill out this field.") never show. `<FieldErrors />` from `src/components/ui/validation.tsx` is mounted once in `src/app/layout.tsx`, next to `<Toaster />`. Don't mount another. Each field that fails a check gets a red edge, a short shake and a warning triangle at its right end. Its message shows in the Hub's own tooltip while the pointer is on the triangle, in the surface's `--pick-*` colours.

- Keep using native constraints: `required`, `type="email"`, `min`/`max`, `minLength`/`maxLength`, `pattern` and `setCustomValidity("…")` for your own rules. Use custom validity messages for format hints instead of native `title` tooltips. Keep `title=""` where needed to suppress a browser's fallback hover tooltip. Don't call `preventDefault` on `invalid` in individual forms or fields, and don't build your own bubble; the shared validation component handles suppression.
- Removing a native tooltip must preserve the warning triangles, red edges, shake animation, accessible messages and focus on the first invalid field. Never disable the shared validation system to hide a tooltip, or replace it with a separate validation implementation.
- On a failed submit, every failing field is marked (`data-invalid`, plus `aria-invalid` when the page hasn't set it) and shakes again. The first one is focused and its message is read out. Each message is linked to its field with `aria-describedby`. The sign, the red edge and the extra right padding that makes room for the sign all go away as soon as the field passes.
- Clicking, tapping or moving keyboard focus into a flagged field clears that field's warning triangle, red edge, shake and message immediately. The automatic focus after a failed submit must keep the feedback visible. Clearing feedback does not make the value valid: submitting again must recheck the constraints and restore feedback for any field that still fails.
- The warning triangle always takes the rightmost slot, 10px inside the field's right edge. Temporarily hide any existing icon, calendar button, clear button, tag or stepper in that slot while the warning is visible. Restore those elements when the user interacts with the field and the warning clears; don't move the triangle to the left to make room for them.
- The default wording is plain, for example "Fill in this field." or "Enter an email address, like name@institution.org." A custom validity message is shown as written. Set `data-missing="…"` on a field to change its empty-field message.
- Don't add `noValidate` or `formNoValidate`, or manually bypass constraint validation, to suppress browser tooltips. All fields must continue to trigger the shared warning triangles and shake feedback. When updating a form with a separate validation implementation, bring its field feedback into the shared system.
- Write each message as a short sentence that says what to do: "Enter a birthday from 01/01/1900 and at least 15 years ago.", not "Invalid input."

## Content tabs use the shared pill style

Content tabs switch the content shown within a page, section, drawer or modal. Use `Tabs` and `TabPanel` from `src/components/ui/interactive.tsx`. Administration's `Tabs` in `src/components/admin/ui.tsx` delegates to the same component. Do not build another tab strip or override its appearance for a page. The shared styles live in `src/styles/content-tabs.css`.

- The entire strip is a rounded pill with a soft layered shadow: 3px padding, 2px between tabs, 30px tab buttons (36px overall), 14px horizontal button padding, 13px regular-weight labels. Keep labels vertically centered and never bold the active label. Long strips scroll horizontally on small screens instead of wrapping or clipping.
- Unit category tabs and headings use exactly `Faculty/Colleges`, `University Offices` and `Research Centers`, in that order. Reuse the labels in `src/lib/unit-categories.ts` in Unit Participation and the Organizational Directory. Research centers have their own Directory section rather than appearing under University Offices. Keep stored organizational kind values unchanged.
- Light theme: white strip and inactive pills with dark text; jet-black (`#1d1d1f`) active pill with white text. Dark theme: jet-black strip and inactive pills with light-gray text; white active pill with dark text. Use the shared `--tabs-*` variables rather than page-specific colors.
- The active highlight slides between tabs by default using Motion's shared layout animation. Keep its existing spring transition and disable movement for reduced-motion preferences. Do not replace it with an underline or a separate selected-button animation.
- Pass plain string tabs, or `{ key, label, count, countTone }` items when values and labels differ. Use `count` or the `counts` prop for badges instead of placing badge markup inside labels. Badges are 16px high with 9px text, centered beside the label with an 8px gap. Normal counts are gold; use `countTone: "alert"` only for actionable alerts, retaining their red fill in both themes.
- Give every strip an accessible `label`. When rendering its content panel, supply a unique `idBase` and use `TabPanel` with the same ID and selected index. Preserve the shared arrow-key, Home/End, roving-focus and selected-state behavior. Keep navigation links, anchored page navigation and form filter controls in their appropriate components.
- Content tabs include their search and page controls as one shared row. Pass the primary search through the `search` prop on `Tabs`, and any page-specific controls through `actions`. The shared layout orders tabs, actions, then search at the far right; do not hand-build separate page-specific tab/search toolbars. On narrow screens the controls may wrap below the tabs. Sections without searchable content may omit `search`.
- Place additional content buttons before the search, including filters, invite/add buttons and overview toggles. Give each trigger `content-tabs__action`; add `content-tabs__action--icon` for a 36px icon-only button and provide an accessible label. All of these buttons match the search: 36px height, full pill shape, 13px regular-weight text, 14px horizontal padding, the same surface fill and soft layered shadow, and a neutral 1px focus outline. Preserve pressed, expanded and disabled states. Do not use a yellow focus glow or a different filled CTA style for controls in this row.
- Use `SearchField` from `src/components/ui/search-field.tsx` for content search (Administration re-exports it). Its optional `examples` cycle through typed search guides every six seconds, including typing, the reading pause and erasing, while empty (even while focused); typing pauses them and clearing resumes them. Its search icon animates on focus and repeats every four seconds until blur. Both animations respect reduced motion. Enable `shortcuts` for the primary content search: `/` focuses it outside editable fields or modal dialogs; Escape clears text, then leaves an empty field. Show the corresponding `/` or `Esc` key hint at the right edge. Never steal typing shortcuts from another field.
